# Changelog

All notable changes to Calendary will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added

- Weekly and date-specific availability schedules.
- Date-specific schedule overrides.
- Configurable slot duration and interval.
- Configurable break time after bookings.
- Busy times and explicit busy periods.
- Days off and holidays.
- Day statuses: `open`, `closed`, `full`, `day_off`, and `holiday`.
- Slot statuses: `available` and `busy`.
- Queries for single days, weeks, and date ranges.
- Filtering by availability and status.
- Field selection with `select()`.
- Available-slot filtering with `availableSlots()`.
- Array and JSON serialization.
- Timezone support.
